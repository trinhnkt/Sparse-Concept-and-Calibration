Next-response correctness is not latent mastery: y=0 is an incorrect next attempt, not a latent-skill diagnosis. The threshold gate is a simulated probe, not a classroom policy, and is not contribution (ii). There is no classroom RCT. We do not train graph, contrastive, or self-supervised KT models. BKT is not a scored baseline; IRT is the classical reference (pyBKT 1.4.1 degenerated on ASSISTments seed 42; Supplementary Table S9). Official SimpleKT [4] is scored on ASSISTments AUC/ECE only and is not compared to a published ASSISTments 2012 pyKT cell, which that paper does not report. Pairwise DeLong tests on AUC are not the main reporting device; we report mean±sd across four unique learner partitions. Temporal evaluation uses a single corrected cutoff (seed 42). Seeds 2025 and 2026 share a split. R/L/I are descriptive support flags. ECE depends on binning. Three datasets cannot establish a universal diagnostic law.
